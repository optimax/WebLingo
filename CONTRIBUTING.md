# Contributing to WebLingo

WebLingo and WASP are in beta. What helps most now is your reports: what you tried, what worked, and what did not.

## Reporting a bug or asking a question

Open an [issue](https://github.com/optimax/WebLingo/issues/new/choose) and choose its form:

- **A bug**, when something does not work as the documents say, or fails: the version, as `weblingo --version` or
  `wasp --version` prints it, the package you installed, your operating system, where it runs, what you did, what you
  expected and what happened. The smallest script, page or site that shows it, with its configuration, helps most.
- **A question**, when you want to know how to do something, or what something means. A question the documents leave
  open tells us what they lack.

Look first at the [open issues](https://github.com/optimax/WebLingo/issues), in case someone has reported it already.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Never in an issue: privately, as the [security policy](SECURITY.md) says.

## Pull requests

Pull requests are not accepted yet, until the project's governance is decided: who reviews and merges, and how a
contribution is licensed. An issue saying what you would change, and why, is welcome meanwhile.

## Building it yourself

[WebLingo/README.md](WebLingo/README.md) says how to build, test and run it from the repository, with the .NET 10 SDK.
